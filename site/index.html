reins. Arc testnet · 26 Sep 2026

Reins is software. Funds placed under a mandate sit in a smart contract controlled by its owner; Reins never has custody of, or access to, user funds. The mandate shown in product panels is an example — tokenized equities such as SPYx are not yet issued on Arc. Every transaction under “Broadcast, not promised” is real and links to the Arc testnet explorer.

Nothing on this page is investment advice or an offer of securities. Automated strategies can lose money up to the loss limit their mandate sets. Figures dated above were read from Arc testnet chain state.
