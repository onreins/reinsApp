Why it holds

Limits enforced by the contract, not promised in a footer.

The rules can't be edited

Size limit, loss limit, which assets, when it expires. Fixed when the mandate is created and immutable after that — by us or by anyone.

You can leave mid-strategy

Withdraw everything at any moment without asking the agent, even while it holds a position. There is no function that lets it object.

The stop-loss is public

If a mandate falls through its floor, any stranger can freeze it. You don't have to be awake for your loss limit to work, and neither do we.
