Reins Live on Arc testnet

Hand an agent the reins. Never the keys.

Run an automated strategy on your money, or follow someone else's, without anyone being able to take it. Your funds sit in your own contract under rules you set. The agent can trade. It has no function that withdraws.

How it holds

A stop-loss written into the contract, not promised in a footer.

A broker's limits are instructions someone honours later. Here they are conditions checked before any money moves. A trade that breaks one reverts, and nothing happens.

Set once

The rules cannot be edited

Size limit, loss limit, which assets, when it expires. Fixed when the mandate is created and immutable afterwards.

Always

You can leave mid-strategy

Withdraw everything at any moment without asking the agent, even while it holds a position. It cannot object.

Anyone

The stop-loss is public

If a mandate falls through its floor, any stranger can freeze it. You do not have to be awake, and neither do we.
